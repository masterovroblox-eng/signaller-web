Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5th September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Signaller Web Investigation System collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. This system is a restricted internal tool for authorised personnel only. We operate in full compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

1. Who Controls Your Data

The data controller for this system is the Signaller Web management team. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) remains:

masterovroblox@gmail.com

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to operate this investigation system:

  • Account Data: Username, system role (supervisor stored as staff, or admin), and a securely hashed password. Plain-text passwords are never stored. Accounts below supervisor are not permitted.
  • Session Tokens: A temporary session to keep you signed in. Tokens expire automatically.
  • Investigation Records: Title, incident date, location, involved parties, description, outcome, findings, recommendations, status (open/closed), opened/closed by, and timestamps. May contain personal data about involved individuals.
  • Audit Log: Record of administrative actions (account creation, password resets, investigation create/update/close) referencing your username for accountability.

We do not collect fleet data, attendance, performance reports, or Discord data in this system.

3. How We Collect Data

  • Information you provide: When you log in or create/update an investigation.
  • Information generated by your use: Audit entries and investigation metadata created automatically when you perform actions.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Account management and incident investigation handling for the legitimate operation of Signaller Web.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Where you voluntarily provide optional information within an investigation record.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • Database provider: Stores accounts, sessions, investigations and audit logs.
  • Web hosting platform: Serves this site.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained until deleted by an admin or a verified erasure request is processed.
  • Investigation records: Retained for accountability. May be anonymised or deleted after closure and resolution.
  • Session tokens: Expire automatically.
  • Audit logs: Retained for security and accountability.

7. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored in plain text.
  • All database connections are encrypted (TLS).
  • Session tokens are random, not guessable.
  • Admin actions are logged.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

  • Right of Access (15): Request a copy of all data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification (16): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (17): Request deletion of your account/records when leaving, subject to legal retention.
  • Right to Restriction (18): Request limited processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (20): Receive your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object (21): Object to processing under legitimate interests.

To exercise rights, contact masterovroblox@gmail.com. We respond within one calendar month.

9. Cookies

We use only essential storage: your session to keep you signed in. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
